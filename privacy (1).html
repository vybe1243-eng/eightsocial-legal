8 Social – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. Contact: eightsocial.app@gmail.com

1. Who we are

8 Social, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, decides how your personal data is used in the app. You can reach us, and our grievance contact, at eightsocial.app@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your name, username, email, account type and sign-in method. Passwords and Google sign-in are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we do not see your plain password. With Google sign-in we receive your name, email and profile photo. When you sign up, we ask for your date of birth once, only to check that you are 18 or older. We do not store your date of birth; we store only a yes/no confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Profile: bio, profile photo, and business details you choose to add (business name and type, phone, email, website, area, other details).
  • Content: posts, Kurals, comments, likes, saved posts, groups you create or join, and ads you create.
  • Messages and calls: the messages you send and call details, stored so they can be delivered to the people you chat with.
  • Location: only with your permission, for features such as Where Am I, directions, nearby places and distance, live location sharing and SOS. You can turn the permission off in your phone settings.
  • Trusted contacts: the names and phone numbers of trusted contacts you add for SOS.
  • Vyro AI: what you type to Vyro, and the last few messages of the current chat so Vyro can follow the conversation, are sent to Google’s Gemini AI service to produce an answer. We do not store your Vyro conversations. If you use voice input, your phone’s speech recognition service (for example Google’s) may process your voice. Please do not share passwords, OTPs, bank or card details or other sensitive information with Vyro.
  • Usage and technical data: basic technical information needed to run and secure the app, and counts of ad views and taps.

3. How we use it

To run your account and the app, show your feed, deliver messages, provide location and safety features, show Sponsored ads, keep the community safe (moderation and handling reports), meet legal duties, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Consent

We use your data based on your consent, which you give when you sign up and when you turn on features such as location. You can withdraw consent by turning permissions off or by deleting your account.

5. Who can see your data

Your public profile, public posts, Kurals and business profile can be seen by other users, as set by your privacy settings. Messages are visible to the people you send them to. If you use SOS or live location, your location goes to the trusted contacts you chose.

6. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and Google Sign-In to run the app. Vyro uses Google’s Gemini AI service through Google Firebase AI Logic. We send only what you type to Vyro and the last few messages of the current chat, never your name, email, posts, messages or location. While we use the free tier of the Gemini service, Google may use what you send to Vyro, and Vyro’s answers, to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Google removes the link to your Google account and to our project before review. We may share data when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety. Your data may be stored on servers outside India.

7. Ads

Ads are labelled “Sponsored”. We do not read your messages to show ads. Advertisers see only counts such as views and taps, not your personal data.

8. How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete it in the app, open Settings > About > Delete account, confirm your password or Google sign-in, and type DELETE. This removes your profile, posts, Kurals, followers and following, groups you own, business details and ads, notifications, saved posts, blocked list and trusted contacts, and then your sign-in account.

Some things may remain after deletion: messages you already sent (the people you chatted with may still see them), comments and likes you made on other people’s content, and your membership records in private groups. Data in backups or logs may take a short time to clear, and we may keep what the law requires us to keep.

If you cannot use the app, you can ask us to delete your account and data by emailing eightsocial.app@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw your consent, or raise a complaint, by emailing eightsocial.app@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India as the law provides.

10. Children

8 Social is only for people aged 18 and older. If we learn that a person under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date above and tell you in the app if the change is important.
